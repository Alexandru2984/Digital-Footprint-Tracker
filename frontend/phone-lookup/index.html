Phone number lookup

Check a phone number in international format: whether it is valid, which carrier and country it belongs to and what kind of line it is.

Run a free scanHow it works

What a lookup returns

  • Validity and whether the number is assigned.
  • Carrier, country and region of the number — the country, the region its prefix was assigned to and the carrier its range was allocated to are answered offline, from Google’s libphonenumber data, even when no provider answers. A ported number may since have moved carrier.
  • Line type: mobile, landline, VoIP, toll-free.
  • Registered name, where the provider has one.

Numbers found during other scans

A phone number on a target’s website (a tel: link) or in a domain registration becomes a lead of its own and is looked up too — but only numbers in international form, since a national number would mean guessing the country. A number read off an account that only shares the target’s name is never added to their profile.

Questions

Which format should I use?

International format with the country code, for example +40 721 234 567 or +1 415 555 0123. A national number such as 0721 234 567 names no country, so the scan asks for the code.

Can I find the owner of a phone number?

Sometimes: the lookup returns a registered name when the provider has one. The number is also matched against what other sources found about a target.
