What the scanner does, what it does not, and how it treats the data.

Questions

What can I search for?

An email address, a username, a domain, an IP address or a phone number in international format. The scanner recognises which one you typed.

Is it free?

Yes. Anonymous scans cover most sources; a free, verified account adds the 480-site username sweep, account registration checks, saved scans and monitoring.

How accurate are the results?

Every finding carries the reason it is connected to the target and an attribution score. Accounts tied by verified or published links are reliable; accounts found by name alone are marked unconfirmed.

Is OSINT legal?

Collecting public information is legal in most places, but how you use it may not be. Scan yourself, your organisation, or people you are authorised to investigate, and follow the acceptable use policy.

Does the person being scanned get notified?

No. Sources are queried passively and account recovery checks never send a reset.

What happens to my scans?

Inputs and results are encrypted at rest. Anonymous scans are deleted after 30 days; account holders choose their own retention.

Do I need API keys?

No. Shodan, AbuseIPDB, VirusTotal and Have I Been Pwned keys add sources when the operator configures them, but the free sources cover every input type.

Can I use it from scripts?

Yes, through the REST API with an API key from your account. See the API documentation.

Is there tracking on this site?

No. There are no third-party scripts, analytics or beacons; every asset is served from this origin.
